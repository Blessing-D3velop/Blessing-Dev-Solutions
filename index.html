How a project runs

    Hi, I'm Blessing Muthivhi

    I'm a freelance web developer based in South Africa who builds websites in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I started in 2025 and have since launched sites for tattoo studios, spas, preschools, barbers and a construction company.

    I handle the full job myself: gathering what you need, designing, building, connecting your domain, hosting on Vercel and getting your site indexed on Google Search Console. I also add WhatsApp booking and PayFast payments so customers can reach you and pay you from the site.

    For online stores, booking systems and sites that need logins, I use Supabase for the database and secure sign-in, so your products, bookings and customer details are stored safely and you can manage them from your own admin dashboard.

    My clients so far are in South Africa, and I'm now taking on clients in other countries. Everything is done remotely, in English, with clear updates along the way.
